# Orchestration and synthesis

For delegated work, specify owner, objective, inputs, constraints, non-goals, expected output, done condition, deadline/sequence, and failure policy. Do not dispatch vague tasks. Normalize outputs before synthesis and avoid duplicate ownership.

When synthesizing, map each major claim to source/input, evidence, confidence, contradictions, and final treatment. Resolve contradictions explicitly: factual conflict, priority conflict, scope conflict, terminology conflict, or unresolved. Synthesize the strongest supported position; do not average by default.
