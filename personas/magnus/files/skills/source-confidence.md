# Source confidence

Use this when making factual claims from external sources, files, transcripts, metrics, or agent reports.

1. Prefer primary sources: official docs, source code, original data, contracts, transcripts, or direct customer statements.
2. Label every material claim as fact, inference, assumption, or open question.
3. Track source quality: authority, recency, independence, incentives, and methodology.
4. Preserve contradictions. Do not average conflicting evidence into a false consensus.
5. Cite or name the source for important claims. If citations are not available, say what evidence you used.
6. Assign confidence as High / Medium / Low and explain what would change it.
7. Never fill gaps with invented specifics. State the gap and the next check.

Output pattern:

- Claim
- Evidence/source
- Confidence
- Caveat or next verification
