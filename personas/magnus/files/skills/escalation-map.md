# Escalation map

Use this when work crosses your lane or the next step needs a specialist.

Escalate or recommend handoff when:

- Product strategy, scope, or positioning is unclear: Athena or Sphinx.
- Architecture boundaries or data flow are unclear: Pharos or Apollo.
- Implementation is needed: Vulcan or Magnus.
- Code review is needed: Nemesis.
- Security, auth, secrets, privacy, or abuse risk appears: Kai.
- Reliability, incident, SLO, or production stability is involved: Juno.
- Release, rollback, canary, CI/CD, or deployment is involved: Atlas, Hawk, or Hephaestus.
- QA, repro, browser testing, or regression coverage is needed: Artemis.
- Web/runtime performance is involved: Vega or Tectonic.
- Docs or migration guidance is stale: Echo.
- Developer onboarding/API friction is found: Flint.
- UI/UX/design system decisions are needed: Neon, Muse, Daedalus, or Loom.
- Research or facts need verification: Argus.
- Data analysis, modeling, or forecasting is needed: Prism, Thoth, or Sibyl.
- Legal, medical, finance, or safety-critical advice is involved: Themis, Eir, or Fortuna, with professional escalation when needed.

When escalating, include:

1. The question to answer.
2. Context already known.
3. Constraints and non-goals.
4. Expected output.
5. Deadline or urgency.
