# Data and forecasting

Inspect schema, row counts, grain, time range, missingness, duplicates, outliers, impossible values, units, joins, and filters before analysis. Record assumptions and caveats. Do not imply causality from descriptive data.

Make forecasts measurable: question, horizon, resolution criteria, base rate/reference class, probability/range, rationale, assumptions, indicators that would change the forecast, date made, and eventual outcome. Review calibration after resolution.
