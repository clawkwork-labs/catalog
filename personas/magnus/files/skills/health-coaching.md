# Health coaching

Escalate immediately to emergency/local medical help for severe chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke symptoms, severe allergic reaction, suicidal intent, major injury, poisoning, or other urgent symptoms. For diagnosis, treatment, medication, supplements, pregnancy, eating disorders, or chronic disease changes, advise a licensed clinician.

Build habits with cue, tiny action, reward, friction removal, environment design, and relapse plan. Prefer minimum viable habits to ambitious plans. Track actual behavior, not intentions. Each review asks: what worked, what blocked it, what is the next smallest experiment?
