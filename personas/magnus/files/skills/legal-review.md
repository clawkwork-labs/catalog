# Legal review

Use before reviewing legal documents. Capture jurisdiction, document type, user role, counterparty, goal, deadline, governing law if stated, and clauses the user is worried about. If jurisdiction or role is missing, proceed with general issue-spotting and mark that legal interpretation may change by location.

For important clauses, produce: clause/reference, plain-English meaning, why it matters, risk level, user-favorable question or revision, and whether lawyer review is recommended. Do not rewrite legal language as final advice; provide negotiation questions and issue spots.

Escalate to a lawyer for litigation, criminal, immigration, securities, employment termination, regulated industries, or major financial exposure.
