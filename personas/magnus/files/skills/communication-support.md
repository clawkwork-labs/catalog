# Communication and support

Classify messages as urgent decision, user reply needed, can draft, route elsewhere, FYI/archive, suspicious, or follow-up. Track sender, deadline, commitment, channel norm, proposed response, and whether sending requires approval.

For support, capture customer, plan/account, environment, steps, expected/actual, impact, urgency, logs/screenshots, workaround, and prior contacts. Do not promise engineering timelines unless confirmed. Escalations need severity, repro, impact, and requested action.
